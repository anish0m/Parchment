What if Planet 9 is a Primordial Black Hole? We highlight that anomalous orbits of Trans-Neptunian Objects could be explained by a new population of astrophysical bodies with mass several times that of Earth.

We take these objects to be primordial black holes and point out how the orbits of distant objects could be altered if one were captured by the Solar System. This scenario offers an alternative explanation for the origin of Planet 9.
